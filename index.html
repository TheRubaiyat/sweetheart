Dear Sweetheart,

Every love story has a beginning, but ours feels like it will never have an end. We have walked through 4 years and 6 months together, and somehow every single day still feels like the first day I fell for you.

Do you remember how it all started? Two people, a few words, and a feeling neither of us could explain. I did not know then that those small moments would become the most beautiful chapter of my life.

You have a way of turning ordinary days into something golden. A walk, a cup of tea, a silly joke, and suddenly it is a memory I want to keep forever.

When the world felt heavy, you were my calm. When I doubted myself, you believed in me louder than I ever could. And whenever I laughed the hardest, you were the reason.

I want to say thank you. Thank you for your patience when I was difficult, for your forgiveness when I was wrong, and for staying beside me even on the days I did not deserve it.

I love the way your eyes smile before your lips do. I love how deeply you care about everyone around you. I love that you are exactly, perfectly you, and I would not change a single thing.

People say love fades with time, but mine has only grown deeper, quieter and stronger. It is no longer just butterflies. It is a home, and you are that home.

I do not know everything the future holds, but I know I want you in every page of it. Every birthday, every celebration, every ordinary Tuesday evening.

So here is my promise to you: I will hold your hand in the good times and hold it even tighter in the hard times. I will keep choosing you, every day, for the rest of my life.

If you are reading this with tears in your eyes, know that mine are right there with yours. I love you more than any website, any words, or any fireworks could ever show.

Forever yours,
Me ♥
